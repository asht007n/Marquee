# Marquee

**A film and TV diary for watching together.** Log what you watch, keep a watchlist, get recommendations from what you've loved, see where anything is streaming, and settle "what are we watching tonight?" with friends in about a minute.

**Try it:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/marquee/

![Marquee home page](screenshot.png)

## Features

- **Diary and ratings** with half stars, reviews, who you watched with, where, and the vibe
- **You may like:** "Because you liked…" picks for films and TV
- **Popular this week** and the **best of every decade** by genre
- **Where to watch** for your country
- **Tonight:** merges your group's watchlists, keeps what's streaming and finishes in time, then runs an anonymous veto round
- **Friends without accounts:** share links, Taste Clash, and merged watchlists
- **Calibrated ratings** that read each person's stars on their own scale
- **Taste map** of your blind spots, plus watchlist, favorites, TV progress, Letterboxd import and backups

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file, with no framework or build step. Data from the TMDB API, saved in each user's browser.
